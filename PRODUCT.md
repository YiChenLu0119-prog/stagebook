# Stagebook product context
Offline blocking and rehearsal editor for musical theatre, plays and student theatre. Success means quickly changing an actor's path and independent timing without moving the playhead or losing lyrics, saving and sharing a portable package, and keeping rehearsal notes usable. English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. GPL-3.0-only. No account, built-in online AI, cloud collaboration or released native desktop installer. Generic public examples only; no private productions or commercial recordings. Version 9.4 adds a lyric-focused singing workspace, role highlighting and pickup loops to the visual editor and four musical-inspired palettes; the product remains a rehearsal preview awaiting broader real-team validation.
